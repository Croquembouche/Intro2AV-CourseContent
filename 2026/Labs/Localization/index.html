What to notice

Algorithms, assumptions, and sources

All scenes are synthetic, use a fixed known map, and estimate (x, y, yaw). ICP uses nearest neighbors and an exact 2D rigid least-squares update. NDT fits regularized cell covariances and minimizes a Gaussian score with an outlier floor; this teaching version uses neighboring cells and coordinate search, not PCL’s Newton / line-search implementation. AMCL uses noisy body-frame odometry, a likelihood-field beam-endpoint model, and KLD adaptive multinomial resampling. Its KLD bins are 0.5 m × 0.5 m × 0.2 rad, with z = 2.326. Automatic recovery injection and production motion-model details are omitted. Global reinitialization is explicit. The true pose generates observations and error readouts; it is never an input to an estimator update.

The particle mean can lie between modes; inspect the particles before trusting that arrow. The NDT score is an optimization cost, not a calibrated pose probability. These demos are not performance comparisons or deployable vehicle localization.
